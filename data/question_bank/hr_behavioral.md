# HR and behavioral interview questions

Q: Tell me about yourself.
A: A strong answer is a 60-90 second story: current role and strengths, one or two relevant achievements, and why this role is the logical next step.

Q: Why do you want this job and company?
A: Connect specific things about the company's product, mission or team to your skills and goals. Show you researched them; avoid generic praise.

Q: Describe a time you faced a conflict with a coworker.
A: Use STAR: a brief situation, your task, the actions you took to listen and reach common ground, and a measurable positive result. Avoid blaming.

Q: Tell me about a failure and what you learned.
A: Pick a real but recoverable mistake, own it, explain the fix you made, and show how you changed your process afterward.

Q: Describe a time you showed leadership without formal authority.
A: Give an example where you rallied people around a goal, set a clear plan, shared credit, and delivered a concrete outcome.

Q: How do you prioritize when everything is urgent?
A: Clarify impact and deadlines with stakeholders, rank by value and effort, communicate trade-offs, and review priorities regularly.

Q: Tell me about a time you handled tight deadlines.
A: Describe how you scoped, cut low-value work, communicated early, and delivered the essential result on time, with numbers if possible.

Q: What is your greatest strength?
A: Name one strength relevant to the job and prove it with a short example and result.

Q: What is your greatest weakness?
A: Share a genuine, non-critical weakness and the concrete steps you are taking to improve, with evidence of progress.

Q: How do you handle receiving critical feedback?
A: Listen without defending, ask clarifying questions, act on it, and follow up. Give an example where feedback improved your work.

Q: Where do you see yourself in five years?
A: Show ambition aligned with the role: growing skills, taking on more responsibility, and contributing to the company's goals.

Q: Do you have any questions for us?
A: Always ask thoughtful questions about team goals, success metrics in the first 90 days, challenges, and growth, not salary or perks first.
